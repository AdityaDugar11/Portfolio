About Aditya Dugar

I'm a first-year engineering student at Jain University with an unrelenting drive to build things that matter. While most are still figuring out the curriculum, I'm deep into building real AI systems — from computer vision pipelines to fully automated content workflows.

AgriVision Pro, my crop disease detection system, puts AI directly in the hands of Indian farmers through WhatsApp. My automation pipelines run entirely locally, generating and posting social media content without any cloud dependency. These aren't classroom exercises — they're working products.

I compete in hackathons not for the certificates, but for the pressure-cooker environments where ideas crystallize into code. Every weekend is a sprint. Every project is a step closer to the future I'm engineering — one commit at a time.
